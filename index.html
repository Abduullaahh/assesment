What is VPS hosting?

A virtual private server gives you reserved CPU, memory and storage inside an isolated environment. It offers more control and predictable performance than shared hosting.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes. You can move to a larger plan as demand grows. A support specialist will confirm the maintenance window and migration steps before making changes.

Which operating systems can I use?

Choose from current Ubuntu, Debian, AlmaLinux and Rocky Linux images. Other compatible Linux distributions can be arranged during provisioning.

How does annual billing work?

You receive 12 months of service for the cost of 10. The cards show the effective monthly amount, while the full annual total is charged upfront.

Is the VPS managed?

Plans are self-managed by default and include infrastructure support. Managed server administration can be quoted separately for teams that need hands-on help.
